## Psalms 50

- 50:5 **God**: Gather my saints together unto me; those that have made a covenant with me by sacrifice.
- 50:7 **God**: Hear, O my people, and I will speak; O Israel, and I will testify against thee: I am God, even thy God.
- 50:8 **God**: I will not reprove thee for thy sacrifices or thy burnt offerings, to have been continually before me.
- 50:9 **God**: I will take no bullock out of thy house, nor he goats out of thy folds.
- 50:10 **God**: For every beast of the forest is mine, and the cattle upon a thousand hills.
- 50:11 **God**: I know all the fowls of the mountains: and the wild beasts of the field are mine.
- 50:12 **God**: If I were hungry, I would not tell thee: for the world is mine, and the fulness thereof.
- 50:13 **God**: Will I eat the flesh of bulls, or drink the blood of goats?
- 50:14 **God**: Offer unto God thanksgiving; and pay thy vows unto the most High:
- 50:15 **God**: And call upon me in the day of trouble: I will deliver thee, and thou shalt glorify me.
- 50:16 **God**: What hast thou to do to declare my statutes, or that thou shouldest take my covenant in thy mouth?
- 50:17 **God**: Seeing thou hatest instruction, and casteth my words behind thee.
- 50:18 **God**: When thou sawest a thief, then thou consentedst with him, and hast been partaker with adulterers.
- 50:19 **God**: Thou givest thy mouth to evil, and thy tongue frameth deceit.
- 50:20 **God**: Thou sittest and speakest against thy brother; thou slanderest thine own mother’s son.
- 50:21 **God**: These things hast thou done, and I kept silence; thou thoughtest that I was altogether such an one as thyself: but I will reprove thee, and set them in order before thine eyes.
- 50:22 **God**: Now consider this, ye that forget God, lest I tear you in pieces, and there be none to deliver.
- 50:23 **God**: Whoso offereth praise glorifieth me: and to him that ordereth his conversation aright will I shew the salvation of God.
