## Leviticus 1

- 1:2 **the LORD**: Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, If any man of you bring an offering unto the LORD, ye shall bring your offering of the cattle, even of the herd, and of the flock.
- 1:3 **the LORD**: If his offering be a burnt sacrifice of the herd, let him offer a male without blemish: he shall offer it of his own voluntary will at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation before the LORD.
- 1:4 **the LORD**: And he shall put his hand upon the head of the burnt offering; and it shall be accepted for him to make atonement for him.
- 1:5 **the LORD**: And he shall kill the bullock before the LORD: and the priests, Aaron’s sons, shall bring the blood, and sprinkle the blood round about upon the altar that is by the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.
- 1:6 **the LORD**: And he shall flay the burnt offering, and cut it into his pieces.
- 1:7 **the LORD**: And the sons of Aaron the priest shall put fire upon the altar, and lay the wood in order upon the fire:
- 1:8 **the LORD**: And the priests, Aaron’s sons, shall lay the parts, the head, and the fat, in order upon the wood that is on the fire which is upon the altar:
- 1:9 **the LORD**: But his inwards and his legs shall he wash in water: and the priest shall burn all on the altar, to be a burnt sacrifice, an offering made by fire, of a sweet savour unto the LORD.
- 1:10 **the LORD**: And if his offering be of the flocks, namely, of the sheep, or of the goats, for a burnt sacrifice; he shall bring it a male without blemish.
- 1:11 **the LORD**: And he shall kill it on the side of the altar northward before the LORD: and the priests, Aaron’s sons, shall sprinkle his blood round about upon the altar.
- 1:12 **the LORD**: And he shall cut it into his pieces, with his head and his fat: and the priest shall lay them in order on the wood that is on the fire which is upon the altar:
- 1:13 **the LORD**: But he shall wash the inwards and the legs with water: and the priest shall bring it all, and burn it upon the altar: it is a burnt sacrifice, an offering made by fire, of a sweet savour unto the LORD.
- 1:14 **the LORD**: And if the burnt sacrifice for his offering to the LORD be of fowls, then he shall bring his offering of turtledoves, or of young pigeons.
- 1:15 **the LORD**: And the priest shall bring it unto the altar, and wring off his head, and burn it on the altar; and the blood thereof shall be wrung out at the side of the altar:
- 1:16 **the LORD**: And he shall pluck away his crop with his feathers, and cast it beside the altar on the east part, by the place of the ashes:
- 1:17 **the LORD**: And he shall cleave it with the wings thereof, but shall not divide it asunder: and the priest shall burn it upon the altar, upon the wood that is upon the fire: it is a burnt sacrifice, an offering made by fire, of a sweet savour unto the LORD.

## Leviticus 4

- 4:2 **the LORD**: Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, If a soul shall sin through ignorance against any of the commandments of the LORD concerning things which ought not to be done, and shall do against any of them:
- 4:3 **the LORD**: If the priest that is anointed do sin according to the sin of the people; then let him bring for his sin, which he hath sinned, a young bullock without blemish unto the LORD for a sin offering.
- 4:4 **the LORD**: And he shall bring the bullock unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation before the LORD; and shall lay his hand upon the bullock’s head, and kill the bullock before the LORD.
- 4:5 **the LORD**: And the priest that is anointed shall take of the bullock’s blood, and bring it to the tabernacle of the congregation:
- 4:6 **the LORD**: And the priest shall dip his finger in the blood, and sprinkle of the blood seven times before the LORD, before the vail of the sanctuary.
- 4:7 **the LORD**: And the priest shall put some of the blood upon the horns of the altar of sweet incense before the LORD, which is in the tabernacle of the congregation; and shall pour all the blood of the bullock at the bottom of the altar of the burnt offering, which is at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.
- 4:8 **the LORD**: And he shall take off from it all the fat of the bullock for the sin offering; the fat that covereth the inwards, and all the fat that is upon the inwards,
- 4:9 **the LORD**: And the two kidneys, and the fat that is upon them, which is by the flanks, and the caul above the liver, with the kidneys, it shall he take away,
- 4:10 **the LORD**: As it was taken off from the bullock of the sacrifice of peace offerings: and the priest shall burn them upon the altar of the burnt offering.
- 4:11 **the LORD**: And the skin of the bullock, and all his flesh, with his head, and with his legs, and his inwards, and his dung,
- 4:12 **the LORD**: Even the whole bullock shall he carry forth without the camp unto a clean place, where the ashes are poured out, and burn him on the wood with fire: where the ashes are poured out shall he be burnt.
- 4:13 **the LORD**: And if the whole congregation of Israel sin through ignorance, and the thing be hid from the eyes of the assembly, and they have done somewhat against any of the commandments of the LORD concerning things which should not be done, and are guilty;
- 4:14 **the LORD**: When the sin, which they have sinned against it, is known, then the congregation shall offer a young bullock for the sin, and bring him before the tabernacle of the congregation.
- 4:15 **the LORD**: And the elders of the congregation shall lay their hands upon the head of the bullock before the LORD: and the bullock shall be killed before the LORD.
- 4:16 **the LORD**: And the priest that is anointed shall bring of the bullock’s blood to the tabernacle of the congregation:
- 4:17 **the LORD**: And the priest shall dip his finger in some of the blood, and sprinkle it seven times before the LORD, even before the vail.
- 4:18 **the LORD**: And he shall put some of the blood upon the horns of the altar which is before the LORD, that is in the tabernacle of the congregation, and shall pour out all the blood at the bottom of the altar of the burnt offering, which is at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.
- 4:19 **the LORD**: And he shall take all his fat from him, and burn it upon the altar.
- 4:20 **the LORD**: And he shall do with the bullock as he did with the bullock for a sin offering, so shall he do with this: and the priest shall make an atonement for them, and it shall be forgiven them.
- 4:21 **the LORD**: And he shall carry forth the bullock without the camp, and burn him as he burned the first bullock: it is a sin offering for the congregation.
- 4:22 **the LORD**: When a ruler hath sinned, and done somewhat through ignorance against any of the commandments of the LORD his God concerning things which should not be done, and is guilty;
- 4:23 **the LORD**: Or if his sin, wherein he hath sinned, come to his knowledge; he shall bring his offering, a kid of the goats, a male without blemish:
- 4:24 **the LORD**: And he shall lay his hand upon the head of the goat, and kill it in the place where they kill the burnt offering before the LORD: it is a sin offering.
- 4:25 **the LORD**: And the priest shall take of the blood of the sin offering with his finger, and put it upon the horns of the altar of burnt offering, and shall pour out his blood at the bottom of the altar of burnt offering.
- 4:26 **the LORD**: And he shall burn all his fat upon the altar, as the fat of the sacrifice of peace offerings: and the priest shall make an atonement for him as concerning his sin, and it shall be forgiven him.
- 4:27 **the LORD**: And if any one of the common people sin through ignorance, while he doeth somewhat against any of the commandments of the LORD concerning things which ought not to be done, and be guilty;
- 4:28 **the LORD**: Or if his sin, which he hath sinned, come to his knowledge: then he shall bring his offering, a kid of the goats, a female without blemish, for his sin which he hath sinned.
- 4:29 **the LORD**: And he shall lay his hand upon the head of the sin offering, and slay the sin offering in the place of the burnt offering.
- 4:30 **the LORD**: And the priest shall take of the blood thereof with his finger, and put it upon the horns of the altar of burnt offering, and shall pour out all the blood thereof at the bottom of the altar.
- 4:31 **the LORD**: And he shall take away all the fat thereof, as the fat is taken away from off the sacrifice of peace offerings; and the priest shall burn it upon the altar for a sweet savour unto the LORD; and the priest shall make an atonement for him, and it shall be forgiven him.
- 4:32 **the LORD**: And if he bring a lamb for a sin offering, he shall bring it a female without blemish.
- 4:33 **the LORD**: And he shall lay his hand upon the head of the sin offering, and slay it for a sin offering in the place where they kill the burnt offering.
- 4:34 **the LORD**: And the priest shall take of the blood of the sin offering with his finger, and put it upon the horns of the altar of burnt offering, and shall pour out all the blood thereof at the bottom of the altar:
- 4:35 **the LORD**: And he shall take away all the fat thereof, as the fat of the lamb is taken away from the sacrifice of the peace offerings; and the priest shall burn them upon the altar, according to the offerings made by fire unto the LORD: and the priest shall make an atonement for his sin that he hath committed, and it shall be forgiven him.

## Leviticus 5

- 5:1 **the LORD**: And if a soul sin, and hear the voice of swearing, and is a witness, whether he hath seen or known of it; if he do not utter it, then he shall bear his iniquity.
- 5:2 **the LORD**: Or if a soul touch any unclean thing, whether it be a carcase of an unclean beast, or a carcase of unclean cattle, or the carcase of unclean creeping things, and if it be hidden from him; he also shall be unclean, and guilty.
- 5:3 **the LORD**: Or if he touch the uncleanness of man, whatsoever uncleanness it be that a man shall be defiled withal, and it be hid from him; when he knoweth of it, then he shall be guilty.
- 5:4 **the LORD**: Or if a soul swear, pronouncing with his lips to do evil, or to do good, whatsoever it be that a man shall pronounce with an oath, and it be hid from him; when he knoweth of it, then he shall be guilty in one of these.
- 5:5 **the LORD**: And it shall be, when he shall be guilty in one of these things, that he shall confess that he hath sinned in that thing:
- 5:6 **the LORD**: And he shall bring his trespass offering unto the LORD for his sin which he hath sinned, a female from the flock, a lamb or a kid of the goats, for a sin offering; and the priest shall make an atonement for him concerning his sin.
- 5:7 **the LORD**: And if he be not able to bring a lamb, then he shall bring for his trespass, which he hath committed, two turtledoves, or two young pigeons, unto the LORD; one for a sin offering, and the other for a burnt offering.
- 5:8 **the LORD**: And he shall bring them unto the priest, who shall offer that which is for the sin offering first, and wring off his head from his neck, but shall not divide it asunder:
- 5:9 **the LORD**: And he shall sprinkle of the blood of the sin offering upon the side of the altar; and the rest of the blood shall be wrung out at the bottom of the altar: it is a sin offering.
- 5:10 **the LORD**: And he shall offer the second for a burnt offering, according to the manner: and the priest shall make an atonement for him for his sin which he hath sinned, and it shall be forgiven him.
- 5:11 **the LORD**: But if he be not able to bring two turtledoves, or two young pigeons, then he that sinned shall bring for his offering the tenth part of an ephah of fine flour for a sin offering; he shall put no oil upon it, neither shall he put any frankincense thereon: for it is a sin offering.
- 5:12 **the LORD**: Then shall he bring it to the priest, and the priest shall take his handful of it, even a memorial thereof, and burn it on the altar, according to the offerings made by fire unto the LORD: it is a sin offering.
- 5:13 **the LORD**: And the priest shall make an atonement for him as touching his sin that he hath sinned in one of these, and it shall be forgiven him: and the remnant shall be the priest’s, as a meat offering.
- 5:15 **the LORD**: If a soul commit a trespass, and sin through ignorance, in the holy things of the LORD; then he shall bring for his trespass unto the LORD a ram without blemish out of the flocks, with thy estimation by shekels of silver, after the shekel of the sanctuary, for a trespass offering:
- 5:16 **the LORD**: And he shall make amends for the harm that he hath done in the holy thing, and shall add the fifth part thereto, and give it unto the priest: and the priest shall make an atonement for him with the ram of the trespass offering, and it shall be forgiven him.
- 5:17 **the LORD**: And if a soul sin, and commit any of these things which are forbidden to be done by the commandments of the LORD; though he wist it not, yet is he guilty, and shall bear his iniquity.
- 5:18 **the LORD**: And he shall bring a ram without blemish out of the flock, with thy estimation, for a trespass offering, unto the priest: and the priest shall make an atonement for him concerning his ignorance wherein he erred and wist it not, and it shall be forgiven him.
- 5:19 **the LORD**: It is a trespass offering: he hath certainly trespassed against the LORD.

## Leviticus 6

- 6:2 **the LORD**: If a soul sin, and commit a trespass against the LORD, and lie unto his neighbour in that which was delivered him to keep, or in fellowship, or in a thing taken away by violence, or hath deceived his neighbour;
- 6:3 **the LORD**: Or have found that which was lost, and lieth concerning it, and sweareth falsely; in any of all these that a man doeth, sinning therein:
- 6:4 **the LORD**: Then it shall be, because he hath sinned, and is guilty, that he shall restore that which he took violently away, or the thing which he hath deceitfully gotten, or that which was delivered him to keep, or the lost thing which he found,
- 6:5 **the LORD**: Or all that about which he hath sworn falsely; he shall even restore it in the principal, and shall add the fifth part more thereto, and give it unto him to whom it appertaineth, in the day of his trespass offering.
- 6:6 **the LORD**: And he shall bring his trespass offering unto the LORD, a ram without blemish out of the flock, with thy estimation, for a trespass offering, unto the priest:
- 6:7 **the LORD**: And the priest shall make an atonement for him before the LORD: and it shall be forgiven him for any thing of all that he hath done in trespassing therein.
- 6:9 **the LORD**: Command Aaron and his sons, saying, This is the law of the burnt offering: It is the burnt offering, because of the burning upon the altar all night unto the morning, and the fire of the altar shall be burning in it.
- 6:10 **the LORD**: And the priest shall put on his linen garment, and his linen breeches shall he put upon his flesh, and take up the ashes which the fire hath consumed with the burnt offering on the altar, and he shall put them beside the altar.
- 6:11 **the LORD**: And he shall put off his garments, and put on other garments, and carry forth the ashes without the camp unto a clean place.
- 6:12 **the LORD**: And the fire upon the altar shall be burning in it; it shall not be put out: and the priest shall burn wood on it every morning, and lay the burnt offering in order upon it; and he shall burn thereon the fat of the peace offerings.
- 6:13 **the LORD**: The fire shall ever be burning upon the altar; it shall never go out.
- 6:14 **the LORD**: And this is the law of the meat offering: the sons of Aaron shall offer it before the LORD, before the altar.
- 6:15 **the LORD**: And he shall take of it his handful, of the flour of the meat offering, and of the oil thereof, and all the frankincense which is upon the meat offering, and shall burn it upon the altar for a sweet savour, even the memorial of it, unto the LORD.
- 6:16 **the LORD**: And the remainder thereof shall Aaron and his sons eat: with unleavened bread shall it be eaten in the holy place; in the court of the tabernacle of the congregation they shall eat it.
- 6:17 **the LORD**: It shall not be baken with leaven. I have given it unto them for their portion of my offerings made by fire; it is most holy, as is the sin offering, and as the trespass offering.
- 6:18 **the LORD**: All the males among the children of Aaron shall eat of it. It shall be a statute for ever in your generations concerning the offerings of the LORD made by fire: every one that toucheth them shall be holy.
- 6:20 **the LORD**: This is the offering of Aaron and of his sons, which they shall offer unto the LORD in the day when he is anointed; the tenth part of an ephah of fine flour for a meat offering perpetual, half of it in the morning, and half thereof at night.
- 6:21 **the LORD**: In a pan it shall be made with oil; and when it is baken, thou shalt bring it in: and the baken pieces of the meat offering shalt thou offer for a sweet savour unto the LORD.
- 6:22 **the LORD**: And the priest of his sons that is anointed in his stead shall offer it: it is a statute for ever unto the LORD; it shall be wholly burnt.
- 6:23 **the LORD**: For every meat offering for the priest shall be wholly burnt: it shall not be eaten.
- 6:25 **the LORD**: Speak unto Aaron and to his sons, saying, This is the law of the sin offering: In the place where the burnt offering is killed shall the sin offering be killed before the LORD: it is most holy.
- 6:26 **the LORD**: The priest that offereth it for sin shall eat it: in the holy place shall it be eaten, in the court of the tabernacle of the congregation.
- 6:27 **the LORD**: Whatsoever shall touch the flesh thereof shall be holy: and when there is sprinkled of the blood thereof upon any garment, thou shalt wash that whereon it was sprinkled in the holy place.
- 6:28 **the LORD**: But the earthen vessel wherein it is sodden shall be broken: and if it be sodden in a brasen pot, it shall be both scoured, and rinsed in water.
- 6:29 **the LORD**: All the males among the priests shall eat thereof: it is most holy.
- 6:30 **the LORD**: And no sin offering, whereof any of the blood is brought into the tabernacle of the congregation to reconcile withal in the holy place, shall be eaten: it shall be burnt in the fire.

## Leviticus 7

- 7:1 **the LORD**: Likewise this is the law of the trespass offering: it is most holy.
- 7:2 **the LORD**: In the place where they kill the burnt offering shall they kill the trespass offering: and the blood thereof shall he sprinkle round about upon the altar.
- 7:3 **the LORD**: And he shall offer of it all the fat thereof; the rump, and the fat that covereth the inwards,
- 7:4 **the LORD**: And the two kidneys, and the fat that is on them, which is by the flanks, and the caul that is above the liver, with the kidneys, it shall he take away:
- 7:5 **the LORD**: And the priest shall burn them upon the altar for an offering made by fire unto the LORD: it is a trespass offering.
- 7:6 **the LORD**: Every male among the priests shall eat thereof: it shall be eaten in the holy place: it is most holy.
- 7:7 **the LORD**: As the sin offering is, so is the trespass offering: there is one law for them: the priest that maketh atonement therewith shall have it.
- 7:8 **the LORD**: And the priest that offereth any man’s burnt offering, even the priest shall have to himself the skin of the burnt offering which he hath offered.
- 7:9 **the LORD**: And all the meat offering that is baken in the oven, and all that is dressed in the fryingpan, and in the pan, shall be the priest’s that offereth it.
- 7:10 **the LORD**: And every meat offering, mingled with oil, and dry, shall all the sons of Aaron have, one as much as another.
- 7:11 **the LORD**: And this is the law of the sacrifice of peace offerings, which he shall offer unto the LORD.
- 7:12 **the LORD**: If he offer it for a thanksgiving, then he shall offer with the sacrifice of thanksgiving unleavened cakes mingled with oil, and unleavened wafers anointed with oil, and cakes mingled with oil, of fine flour, fried.
- 7:13 **the LORD**: Besides the cakes, he shall offer for his offering leavened bread with the sacrifice of thanksgiving of his peace offerings.
- 7:14 **the LORD**: And of it he shall offer one out of the whole oblation for an heave offering unto the LORD, and it shall be the priest’s that sprinkleth the blood of the peace offerings.
- 7:15 **the LORD**: And the flesh of the sacrifice of his peace offerings for thanksgiving shall be eaten the same day that it is offered; he shall not leave any of it until the morning.
- 7:16 **the LORD**: But if the sacrifice of his offering be a vow, or a voluntary offering, it shall be eaten the same day that he offereth his sacrifice: and on the morrow also the remainder of it shall be eaten:
- 7:17 **the LORD**: But the remainder of the flesh of the sacrifice on the third day shall be burnt with fire.
- 7:18 **the LORD**: And if any of the flesh of the sacrifice of his peace offerings be eaten at all on the third day, it shall not be accepted, neither shall it be imputed unto him that offereth it: it shall be an abomination, and the soul that eateth of it shall bear his iniquity.
- 7:19 **the LORD**: And the flesh that toucheth any unclean thing shall not be eaten; it shall be burnt with fire: and as for the flesh, all that be clean shall eat thereof.
- 7:20 **the LORD**: But the soul that eateth of the flesh of the sacrifice of peace offerings, that pertain unto the LORD, having his uncleanness upon him, even that soul shall be cut off from his people.
- 7:21 **the LORD**: Moreover the soul that shall touch any unclean thing, as the uncleanness of man, or any unclean beast, or any abominable unclean thing, and eat of the flesh of the sacrifice of peace offerings, which pertain unto the LORD, even that soul shall be cut off from his people.
- 7:23 **the LORD**: Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, Ye shall eat no manner of fat, of ox, or of sheep, or of goat.
- 7:24 **the LORD**: And the fat of the beast that dieth of itself, and the fat of that which is torn with beasts, may be used in any other use: but ye shall in no wise eat of it.
- 7:25 **the LORD**: For whosoever eateth the fat of the beast, of which men offer an offering made by fire unto the LORD, even the soul that eateth it shall be cut off from his people.
- 7:26 **the LORD**: Moreover ye shall eat no manner of blood, whether it be of fowl or of beast, in any of your dwellings.
- 7:27 **the LORD**: Whatsoever soul it be that eateth any manner of blood, even that soul shall be cut off from his people.
- 7:29 **the LORD**: Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, He that offereth the sacrifice of his peace offerings unto the LORD shall bring his oblation unto the LORD of the sacrifice of his peace offerings.
- 7:30 **the LORD**: His own hands shall bring the offerings of the LORD made by fire, the fat with the breast, it shall he bring, that the breast may be waved for a wave offering before the LORD.
- 7:31 **the LORD**: And the priest shall burn the fat upon the altar: but the breast shall be Aaron’s and his sons’.
- 7:32 **the LORD**: And the right shoulder shall ye give unto the priest for an heave offering of the sacrifices of your peace offerings.
- 7:33 **the LORD**: He among the sons of Aaron, that offereth the blood of the peace offerings, and the fat, shall have the right shoulder for his part.
- 7:34 **the LORD**: For the wave breast and the heave shoulder have I taken of the children of Israel from off the sacrifices of their peace offerings, and have given them unto Aaron the priest and unto his sons by a statute for ever from among the children of Israel.

## Leviticus 11

- 11:2 **the LORD**: Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, These are the beasts which ye shall eat among all the beasts that are on the earth.
- 11:3 **the LORD**: Whatsoever parteth the hoof, and is clovenfooted, and cheweth the cud, among the beasts, that shall ye eat.
- 11:4 **the LORD**: Nevertheless these shall ye not eat of them that chew the cud, or of them that divide the hoof: as the camel, because he cheweth the cud, but divideth not the hoof; he is unclean unto you.
- 11:5 **the LORD**: And the coney, because he cheweth the cud, but divideth not the hoof; he is unclean unto you.
- 11:6 **the LORD**: And the hare, because he cheweth the cud, but divideth not the hoof; he is unclean unto you.
- 11:7 **the LORD**: And the swine, though he divide the hoof, and be clovenfooted, yet he cheweth not the cud; he is unclean to you.
- 11:8 **the LORD**: Of their flesh shall ye not eat, and their carcase shall ye not touch; they are unclean to you.
- 11:9 **the LORD**: These shall ye eat of all that are in the waters: whatsoever hath fins and scales in the waters, in the seas, and in the rivers, them shall ye eat.
- 11:10 **the LORD**: And all that have not fins and scales in the seas, and in the rivers, of all that move in the waters, and of any living thing which is in the waters, they shall be an abomination unto you:
- 11:11 **the LORD**: They shall be even an abomination unto you; ye shall not eat of their flesh, but ye shall have their carcases in abomination.
- 11:12 **the LORD**: Whatsoever hath no fins nor scales in the waters, that shall be an abomination unto you.
- 11:13 **the LORD**: And these are they which ye shall have in abomination among the fowls; they shall not be eaten, they are an abomination: the eagle, and the ossifrage, and the ospray,
- 11:14 **the LORD**: And the vulture, and the kite after his kind;
- 11:15 **the LORD**: Every raven after his kind;
- 11:16 **the LORD**: And the owl, and the night hawk, and the cuckow, and the hawk after his kind,
- 11:17 **the LORD**: And the little owl, and the cormorant, and the great owl,
- 11:18 **the LORD**: And the swan, and the pelican, and the gier eagle,
- 11:19 **the LORD**: And the stork, the heron after her kind, and the lapwing, and the bat.
- 11:20 **the LORD**: All fowls that creep, going upon all four, shall be an abomination unto you.
- 11:21 **the LORD**: Yet these may ye eat of every flying creeping thing that goeth upon all four, which have legs above their feet, to leap withal upon the earth;
- 11:22 **the LORD**: Even these of them ye may eat; the locust after his kind, and the bald locust after his kind, and the beetle after his kind, and the grasshopper after his kind.
- 11:23 **the LORD**: But all other flying creeping things, which have four feet, shall be an abomination unto you.
- 11:24 **the LORD**: And for these ye shall be unclean: whosoever toucheth the carcase of them shall be unclean until the even.
- 11:25 **the LORD**: And whosoever beareth ought of the carcase of them shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even.
- 11:26 **the LORD**: The carcases of every beast which divideth the hoof, and is not clovenfooted, nor cheweth the cud, are unclean unto you: every one that toucheth them shall be unclean.
- 11:27 **the LORD**: And whatsoever goeth upon his paws, among all manner of beasts that go on all four, those are unclean unto you: whoso toucheth their carcase shall be unclean until the even.
- 11:28 **the LORD**: And he that beareth the carcase of them shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even: they are unclean unto you.
- 11:29 **the LORD**: These also shall be unclean unto you among the creeping things that creep upon the earth; the weasel, and the mouse, and the tortoise after his kind,
- 11:30 **the LORD**: And the ferret, and the chameleon, and the lizard, and the snail, and the mole.
- 11:31 **the LORD**: These are unclean to you among all that creep: whosoever doth touch them, when they be dead, shall be unclean until the even.
- 11:32 **the LORD**: And upon whatsoever any of them, when they are dead, doth fall, it shall be unclean; whether it be any vessel of wood, or raiment, or skin, or sack, whatsoever vessel it be, wherein any work is done, it must be put into water, and it shall be unclean until the even; so it shall be cleansed.
- 11:33 **the LORD**: And every earthen vessel, whereinto any of them falleth, whatsoever is in it shall be unclean; and ye shall break it.
- 11:34 **the LORD**: Of all meat which may be eaten, that on which such water cometh shall be unclean: and all drink that may be drunk in every such vessel shall be unclean.
- 11:35 **the LORD**: And every thing whereupon any part of their carcase falleth shall be unclean; whether it be oven, or ranges for pots, they shall be broken down: for they are unclean, and shall be unclean unto you.
- 11:36 **the LORD**: Nevertheless a fountain or pit, wherein there is plenty of water, shall be clean: but that which toucheth their carcase shall be unclean.
- 11:37 **the LORD**: And if any part of their carcase fall upon any sowing seed which is to be sown, it shall be clean.
- 11:38 **the LORD**: But if any water be put upon the seed, and any part of their carcase fall thereon, it shall be unclean unto you.
- 11:39 **the LORD**: And if any beast, of which ye may eat, die; he that toucheth the carcase thereof shall be unclean until the even.

- 23:28 **the LORD**: And ye shall do no work in that same day: for it is a day of atonement, to make an atonement for you before the LORD your God.
- 23:29 **the LORD**: For whatsoever soul it be that shall not be afflicted in that same day, he shall be cut off from among his people.
- 23:30 **the LORD**: And whatsoever soul it be that doeth any work in that same day, the same soul will I destroy from among his people.

- 23:32 **the LORD**: It shall be unto you a sabbath of rest, and ye shall afflict your souls: in the ninth day of the month at even, from even unto even, shall ye celebrate your sabbath.

- 23:35 **the LORD**: On the first day shall be an holy convocation: ye shall do no servile work therein.
- 23:36 **the LORD**: Seven days ye shall offer an offering made by fire unto the LORD: on the eighth day shall be an holy convocation unto you; and ye shall offer an offering made by fire unto the LORD: it is a solemn assembly; and ye shall do no servile work therein.
- 23:37 **the LORD**: These are the feasts of the LORD, which ye shall proclaim to be holy convocations, to offer an offering made by fire unto the LORD, a burnt offering, and a meat offering, a sacrifice, and drink offerings, every thing upon his day:

- 23:42 **the LORD**: Ye shall dwell in booths seven days; all that are Israelites born shall dwell in booths:
- 23:43 **the LORD**: That your generations may know that I made the children of Israel to dwell in booths, when I brought them out of the land of Egypt: I am the LORD your God.

## Leviticus 25

- 25:2 **the LORD**: Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, When ye come into the land which I give you, then shall the land keep a sabbath unto the LORD.
- 25:3 **the LORD**: Six years thou shalt sow thy field, and six years thou shalt prune thy vineyard, and gather in the fruit thereof;
- 25:4 **the LORD**: But in the seventh year shall be a sabbath of rest unto the land, a sabbath for the LORD: thou shalt neither sow thy field, nor prune thy vineyard.
- 25:5 **the LORD**: That which groweth of its own accord of thy harvest thou shalt not reap, neither gather the grapes of thy vine undressed: for it is a year of rest unto the land.
- 25:6 **the LORD**: And the sabbath of the land shall be meat for you; for thee, and for thy servant, and for thy maid, and for thy hired servant, and for thy stranger that sojourneth with thee,
- 25:7 **the LORD**: And for thy cattle, and for the beast that are in thy land, shall all the increase thereof be meat.
- 25:8 **the LORD**: And thou shalt number seven sabbaths of years unto thee, seven times seven years; and the space of the seven sabbaths of years shall be unto thee forty and nine years.
- 25:9 **the LORD**: Then shalt thou cause the trumpet of the jubile to sound on the tenth day of the seventh month, in the day of atonement shall ye make the trumpet sound throughout all your land.
- 25:10 **the LORD**: And ye shall hallow the fiftieth year, and proclaim liberty throughout all the land unto all the inhabitants thereof: it shall be a jubile unto you; and ye shall return every man unto his possession, and ye shall return every man unto his family.
- 25:11 **the LORD**: A jubile shall that fiftieth year be unto you: ye shall not sow, neither reap that which groweth of itself in it, nor gather the grapes in it of thy vine undressed.
- 25:12 **the LORD**: For it is the jubile; it shall be holy unto you: ye shall eat the increase thereof out of the field.
- 25:13 **the LORD**: In the year of this jubile ye shall return every man unto his possession.
- 25:14 **the LORD**: And if thou sell ought unto thy neighbour, or buyest ought of thy neighbour’s hand, ye shall not oppress one another:
- 25:15 **the LORD**: According to the number of years after the jubile thou shalt buy of thy neighbour, and according unto the number of years of the fruits he shall sell unto thee:
- 25:16 **the LORD**: According to the multitude of years thou shalt increase the price thereof, and according to the fewness of years thou shalt diminish the price of it: for according to the number of the years of the fruits doth he sell unto thee.
- 25:17 **the LORD**: Ye shall not therefore oppress one another; but thou shalt fear thy God:for I am the LORD your God.
- 25:18 **the LORD**: Wherefore ye shall do my statutes, and keep my judgments, and do them; and ye shall dwell in the land in safety.
- 25:19 **the LORD**: And the land shall yield her fruit, and ye shall eat your fill, and dwell therein in safety.
- 25:20 **the LORD**: And if ye shall say, What shall we eat the seventh year? behold, we shall not sow, nor gather in our increase:
- 25:21 **the LORD**: Then I will command my blessing upon you in the sixth year, and it shall bring forth fruit for three years.
- 25:22 **the LORD**: And ye shall sow the eighth year, and eat yet of old fruit until the ninth year; until her fruits come in ye shall eat of the old store.
- 25:23 **the LORD**: The land shall not be sold for ever: for the land is mine; for ye are strangers and sojourners with me.
- 25:24 **the LORD**: And in all the land of your possession ye shall grant a redemption for the land.

- 25:26 **the LORD**: And if the man have none to redeem it, and himself be able to redeem it;
- 25:27 **the LORD**: Then let him count the years of the sale thereof, and restore the overplus unto the man to whom he sold it; that he may return unto his possession.
- 25:28 **the LORD**: But if he be not able to restore it to him, then that which is sold shall remain in the hand of him that hath bought it until the year of jubile: and in the jubile it shall go out, and he shall return unto his possession.
- 25:29 **the LORD**: And if a man sell a dwelling house in a walled city, then he may redeem it within a whole year after it is sold; within a full year may he redeem it.
- 25:30 **the LORD**: And if it be not redeemed within the space of a full year, then the house that is in the walled city shall be established for ever to him that bought it throughout his generations: it shall not go out in the jubile.
- 25:31 **the LORD**: But the houses of the villages which have no wall round about them shall be counted as the fields of the country: they may be redeemed, and they shall go out in the jubile.
- 25:32 **the LORD**: Notwithstanding the cities of the Levites, and the houses of the cities of their possession, may the Levites redeem at any time.
- 25:33 **the LORD**: And if a man purchase of the Levites, then the house that was sold, and the city of his possession, shall go out in the year of jubile: for the houses of the cities of the Levites are their possession among the children of Israel.

- 25:42 **the LORD**: For they are my servants, which I brought forth out of the land of Egypt: they shall not be sold as bondmen.
