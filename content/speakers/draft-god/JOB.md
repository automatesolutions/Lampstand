## Job 1

- 1:7 **the LORD**: Whence comest thou?
- 1:8 **the LORD**: Hast thou considered my servant Job, that there is none like him in the earth, a perfect and an upright man, one that feareth God, and escheweth evil?
- 1:12 **the LORD**: Behold, all that he hath is in thy power; only upon himself put not forth thine hand.

## Job 2

- 2:2 **the LORD**: From whence comest thou?
- 2:3 **the LORD**: Hast thou considered my servant Job, that there is none like him in the earth, a perfect and an upright man, one that feareth God, and escheweth evil? and still he holdeth fast his integrity, although thou movedst me against him, to destroy him without cause.
- 2:6 **the LORD**: Behold, he is in thine hand; but save his life.

## Job 38

- 38:2 **the LORD**: Who is this that darkeneth counsel by words without knowledge?
- 38:3 **the LORD**: Gird up now thy loins like a man; for I will demand of thee, and answer thou me.
- 38:4 **the LORD**: Where wast thou when I laid the foundations of the earth? declare, if thou hast understanding.
- 38:5 **the LORD**: Who hath laid the measures thereof, if thou knowest? or who hath stretched the line upon it?
- 38:6 **the LORD**: Whereupon are the foundations thereof fastened? or who laid the corner stone thereof;
- 38:7 **the LORD**: When the morning stars sang together, and all the sons of God shouted for joy?
- 38:8 **the LORD**: Or who shut up the sea with doors, when it brake forth, as if it had issued out of the womb?
- 38:9 **the LORD**: When I made the cloud the garment thereof, and thick darkness a swaddlingband for it,
- 38:10 **the LORD**: And brake up for it my decreed place, and set bars and doors,
- 38:11 **the LORD**: And said, Hitherto shalt thou come, but no further: and here shall thy proud waves be stayed?
- 38:12 **the LORD**: Hast thou commanded the morning since thy days; and caused the dayspring to know his place;
- 38:13 **the LORD**: That it might take hold of the ends of the earth, that the wicked might be shaken out of it?
- 38:14 **the LORD**: It is turned as clay to the seal; and they stand as a garment.
- 38:15 **the LORD**: And from the wicked their light is withholden, and the high arm shall be broken.
- 38:16 **the LORD**: Hast thou entered into the springs of the sea? or hast thou walked in the search of the depth?
- 38:17 **the LORD**: Have the gates of death been opened unto thee? or hast thou seen the doors of the shadow of death?
- 38:18 **the LORD**: Hast thou perceived the breadth of the earth? declare if thou knowest it all.
- 38:19 **the LORD**: Where is the way where light dwelleth? and as for darkness, where is the place thereof,
- 38:20 **the LORD**: That thou shouldest take it to the bound thereof, and that thou shouldest know the paths to the house thereof?
- 38:21 **the LORD**: Knowest thou it, because thou wast then born? or because the number of thy days is great?
- 38:22 **the LORD**: Hast thou entered into the treasures of the snow? or hast thou seen the treasures of the hail,
- 38:23 **the LORD**: Which I have reserved against the time of trouble, against the day of battle and war?
- 38:24 **the LORD**: By what way is the light parted, which scattereth the east wind upon the earth?
- 38:25 **the LORD**: Who hath divided a watercourse for the overflowing of waters, or a way for the lightning of thunder;
- 38:26 **the LORD**: To cause it to rain on the earth, where no man is; on the wilderness, wherein there is no man;
- 38:27 **the LORD**: To satisfy the desolate and waste ground; and to cause the bud of the tender herb to spring forth?
- 38:28 **the LORD**: Hath the rain a father? or who hath begotten the drops of dew?
- 38:29 **the LORD**: Out of whose womb came the ice? and the hoary frost of heaven, who hath gendered it?
- 38:30 **the LORD**: The waters are hid as with a stone, and the face of the deep is frozen.
- 38:31 **the LORD**: Canst thou bind the sweet influences of Pleiades, or loose the bands of Orion?
- 38:32 **the LORD**: Canst thou bring forth Mazzaroth in his season? or canst thou guide Arcturus with his sons?
- 38:33 **the LORD**: Knowest thou the ordinances of heaven? canst thou set the dominion thereof in the earth?
- 38:34 **the LORD**: Canst thou lift up thy voice to the clouds, that abundance of waters may cover thee?
- 38:35 **the LORD**: Canst thou send lightnings, that they may go, and say unto thee, Here we are?
- 38:36 **the LORD**: Who hath put wisdom in the inward parts? or who hath given understanding to the heart?
- 38:37 **the LORD**: Who can number the clouds in wisdom? or who can stay the bottles of heaven,
- 38:38 **the LORD**: When the dust groweth into hardness, and the clods cleave fast together?
- 38:39 **the LORD**: Wilt thou hunt the prey for the lion? or fill the appetite of the young lions,
- 38:40 **the LORD**: When they couch in their dens, and abide in the covert to lie in wait?
- 38:41 **the LORD**: Who provideth for the raven his food? when his young ones cry unto God, they wander for lack of meat.

## Job 40

- 40:2 **the LORD**: Shall he that contendeth with the Almighty instruct him? he that reproveth God, let him answer it.
- 40:7 **the LORD**: Gird up thy loins now like a man: I will demand of thee, and declare thou unto me.
- 40:8 **the LORD**: Wilt thou also disannul my judgment? wilt thou condemn me, that thou mayest be righteous?
- 40:9 **the LORD**: Hast thou an arm like God? or canst thou thunder with a voice like him?
- 40:10 **the LORD**: Deck thyself now with majesty and excellency; and array thyself with glory and beauty.
- 40:11 **the LORD**: Cast abroad the rage of thy wrath: and behold every one that is proud, and abase him.
- 40:12 **the LORD**: Look on every one that is proud, and bring him low; and tread down the wicked in their place.
- 40:13 **the LORD**: Hide them in the dust together; and bind their faces in secret.
- 40:14 **the LORD**: Then will I also confess unto thee that thine own right hand can save thee.
- 40:15 **the LORD**: Behold now behemoth, which I made with thee; he eateth grass as an ox.
- 40:16 **the LORD**: Lo now, his strength is in his loins, and his force is in the navel of his belly.
- 40:17 **the LORD**: He moveth his tail like a cedar: the sinews of his stones are wrapped together.
- 40:18 **the LORD**: His bones are as strong pieces of brass; his bones are like bars of iron.
- 40:19 **the LORD**: He is the chief of the ways of God: he that made him can make his sword to approach unto him.
- 40:20 **the LORD**: Surely the mountains bring him forth food, where all the beasts of the field play.
- 40:21 **the LORD**: He lieth under the shady trees, in the covert of the reed, and fens.
- 40:22 **the LORD**: The shady trees cover him with their shadow; the willows of the brook compass him about.
- 40:23 **the LORD**: Behold, he drinketh up a river, and hasteth not: he trusteth that he can draw up Jordan into his mouth.
- 40:24 **the LORD**: He taketh it with his eyes: his nose pierceth through snares.

## Job 42

- 42:7 **the LORD**: My wrath is kindled against thee, and against thy two friends: for ye have not spoken of me the thing that is right, as my servant Job hath.
- 42:8 **the LORD**: Therefore take unto you now seven bullocks and seven rams, and go to my servant Job, and offer up for yourselves a burnt offering; and my servant Job shall pray for you: for him will I accept: lest I deal with you after your folly, in that ye have not spoken of me the thing which is right, like my servant Job.
