## Hosea 1

- 1:2 **the LORD**: Go, take unto thee a wife of whoredoms and children of whoredoms: for the land hath committed great whoredom, departing from the LORD.
- 1:4 **the LORD**: Call his name Jezreel; for yet a little while, and I will avenge the blood of Jezreel upon the house of Jehu, and will cause to cease the kingdom of the house of Israel.
- 1:5 **the LORD**: And it shall come to pass at that day, that I will break the bow of Israel in the valley of Jezreel.
- 1:6 **God**: Call her name Loruhamah: for I will no more have mercy upon the house of Israel; but I will utterly take them away.
- 1:7 **God**: But I will have mercy upon the house of Judah, and will save them by the LORD their God, and will not save them by bow, nor by sword, nor by battle, by horses, nor by horsemen.
- 1:9 **God**: Call his name Loammi: for ye are not my people, and I will not be your God.

## Hosea 3

- 3:1 **the LORD**: Go yet, love a woman beloved of her friend, yet an adulteress, according to the love of the LORD toward the children of Israel, who look to other gods, and love flagons of wine.
