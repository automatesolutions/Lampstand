## Zechariah 1

- 1:2 **the LORD**: The LORD hath been sore displeased with your fathers.
- 1:3 **the LORD**: Therefore say thou unto them, Thus saith the LORD of hosts; Turn ye unto me, saith the LORD of hosts, and I will turn unto you,
- 1:4 **the LORD**: Be ye not as your fathers, unto whom the former prophets have cried, saying, Thus saith the LORD of hosts; Turn ye now from your evil ways, and from your evil doings: but they did not hear, nor hearken unto me,
- 1:5 **the LORD**: Your fathers, where are they? and the prophets, do they live for ever?
- 1:6 **the LORD**: But my words and my statutes, which I commanded my servants the prophets, did they not take hold of your fathers? and they returned and said, Like as the LORD of hosts thought to do unto us, according to our ways, and according to our doings, so hath he dealt with us.

## Zechariah 7

- 7:5 **the LORD**: Speak unto all the people of the land, and to the priests, saying, When ye fasted and mourned in the fifth and seventh month, even those seventy years, did ye at all fast unto me, even to me?
- 7:6 **the LORD**: And when ye did eat, and when ye did drink, did not ye eat for yourselves, and drink for yourselves?
- 7:7 **the LORD**: Should ye not hear the words which the LORD hath cried by the former prophets, when Jerusalem was inhabited and in prosperity, and the cities thereof round about her, when men inhabited the south and the plain?
- 7:9 **the LORD**: Execute true judgment, and shew mercy and compassions every man to his brother:
- 7:10 **the LORD**: And oppress not the widow, nor the fatherless, the stranger, nor the poor; and let none of you imagine evil against his brother in your heart.
- 7:11 **the LORD**: But they refused to hearken, and pulled away the shoulder, and stopped their ears, that they should not hear.
- 7:12 **the LORD**: Yea, they made their hearts as an adamant stone, lest they should hear the law, and the words which the LORD of hosts hath sent in his spirit by the former prophets: therefore came a great wrath from the LORD of hosts.
- 7:13 **the LORD**: Therefore it is come to pass, that as he cried, and they would not hear; so they cried, and I would not hear,
- 7:14 **the LORD**: But I scattered them with a whirlwind among all the nations whom they knew not. Thus the land was desolate after them, that no man passed through nor returned: for they laid the pleasant land desolate.
