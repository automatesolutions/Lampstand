## Isaiah 7

- 7:3 **the LORD**: Go forth now to meet Ahaz, thou, and Shearjashub thy son, at the end of the conduit of the upper pool in the highway of the fuller’s field;
- 7:4 **the LORD**: And say unto him, Take heed, and be quiet; fear not, neither be fainthearted for the two tails of these smoking firebrands, for the fierce anger of Rezin with Syria, and of the son of Remaliah.
- 7:5 **the LORD**: Because Syria, Ephraim, and the son of Remaliah, have taken evil counsel against thee, saying,
- 7:6 **the LORD**: Let us go up against Judah, and vex it, and let us make a breach therein for us, and set a king in the midst of it, even the son of Tabeal:
- 7:7 **the LORD**: Thus saith the Lord GOD, It shall not stand, neither shall it come to pass.
- 7:8 **the LORD**: For the head of Syria is Damascus, and the head of Damascus is Rezin; and within threescore and five years shall Ephraim be broken, that it be not a people.
- 7:9 **the LORD**: And the head of Ephraim is Samaria, and the head of Samaria is Remaliah’s son. If ye will not believe, surely ye shall not be established.
- 7:11 **the LORD**: Ask thee a sign of the LORD thy God; ask it either in the depth, or in the height above.

## Isaiah 8

- 8:1 **the LORD**: Take thee a great roll, and write in it with a man’s pen concerning Mahershalalhashbaz.
- 8:3 **the LORD**: Call his name Mahershalalhashbaz.
- 8:4 **the LORD**: For before the child shall have knowledge to cry, My father, and my mother, the riches of Damascus and the spoil of Samaria shall be taken away before the king of Assyria.
- 8:6 **the LORD**: Forasmuch as this people refuseth the waters of Shiloah that go softly, and rejoice in Rezin and Remaliah’s son;
- 8:7 **the LORD**: Now therefore, behold, the Lord bringeth up upon them the waters of the river, strong and many, even the king of Assyria, and all his glory: and he shall come up over all his channels, and go over all his banks:
- 8:8 **the LORD**: And he shall pass through Judah; he shall overflow and go over, he shall reach even to the neck; and the stretching out of his wings shall fill the breadth of thy land, O Immanuel.
- 8:12 **the LORD**: Say ye not, A confederacy, to all them to whom this people shall say, A confederacy; neither fear ye their fear, nor be afraid.
- 8:13 **the LORD**: Sanctify the LORD of hosts himself; and let him be your fear, and let him be your dread.
- 8:14 **the LORD**: And he shall be for a sanctuary; but for a stone of stumbling and for a rock of offence to both the houses of Israel, for a gin and for a snare to the inhabitants of Jerusalem.
- 8:15 **the LORD**: And many among them shall stumble, and fall, and be broken, and be snared, and be taken.

## Isaiah 18

- 18:4 **the LORD**: I will take my rest, and I will consider in my dwelling place like a clear heat upon herbs, and like a cloud of dew in the heat of harvest.

## Isaiah 20

- 20:2 **the LORD**: Go and loose the sackcloth from off thy loins, and put off thy shoe from thy foot.
- 20:3 **the LORD**: Like as my servant Isaiah hath walked naked and barefoot three years for a sign and wonder upon Egypt and upon Ethiopia;
- 20:4 **the LORD**: So shall the king of Assyria lead away the Egyptians prisoners, and the Ethiopians captives, young and old, naked and barefoot, even with their buttocks uncovered, to the shame of Egypt.
- 20:5 **the LORD**: And they shall be afraid and ashamed of Ethiopia their expectation, and of Egypt their glory.
- 20:6 **the LORD**: And the inhabitant of this isle shall say in that day, Behold, such is our expectation, whither we flee for help to be delivered from the king of Assyria: and how shall we escape?
